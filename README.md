# 🌧️ JalMitra – AI-Powered Rainwater Harvesting Platform

JalMitra is a full-stack SaaS platform that helps users analyze the feasibility of rainwater harvesting systems using real environmental data, AI insights, and financial calculations.

It provides instant, data-driven recommendations including ROI, payback period, and water demand vs. supply simulations — making sustainable decision-making faster and more accessible.

## ✨ What JalMitra Does

Simply enter your city, roof area, and household size — JalMitra handles the rest.

It provides:

✦ **Complete Feasibility Report**  
Understand whether rainwater harvesting is actually viable for your home.

✦ **Water Harvesting Insights**  
Get precise estimates of how much water you can collect and how much you can save.

✦ **ROI & Payback Analysis**  
Know exactly how long it takes for your investment to pay off.

✦ **Monthly Breakdown**  
Detailed month-by-month rainfall and harvesting projections.

✦ **AI-Powered Chatbot**  
Interact with your data — ask questions, explore insights, and run what-if scenarios.

✦ **Downloadable Reports**  
Generate ready-to-use reports for contractors, architects, or municipal approvals.

## 🎯 Why I Built This

Water scarcity is a growing issue, yet adoption of rainwater harvesting remains low due to lack of awareness and complex calculations.

JalMitra simplifies this by turning environmental and financial data into actionable insights, helping individuals and organizations make informed sustainability decisions.
